# Full subset privacy also limits correlated quadratic point labels

The [correlated-label analysis](../../research/pointlocks-2026-09-17/correlated-quadratic-labels.md)
extends the earlier independent-coordinate DH boundary. Candidates may now
be arbitrary public linear forms in a smaller collection of hidden scalars.
Requiring every t-subset to leave every unselected scalar unavailable forces
every t+1 candidate forms to be independent.

In the stated algebraic point-evaluation model, a quadratic label component
can be reconstructed only if its quadratic part vanishes on the unopened
fiber. If every t-subset reconstructs one of two fixed nonpublic labels,
choose a nonzero quadratic component from each. Their degree-four product
belongs to every ideal generated by a t-subset of candidate forms. The
minimum degree of that intersection is N-t+1, so **N-t<=3** is necessary.
Vector-valued labels do not remove this obstruction.

This excludes a direct correlated quadratic replacement for the full 5-of-54
interface. Under the analogous leading-form premise, two higher-degree
labels need degree sum at least 50; at least one degree is 25 or higher.
That is a necessary condition, not an efficient higher-degree construction.

A correlated N=t+3 construction reaches the binary-classifier boundary:
use evaluations of a secret degree-t polynomial and two vectors of within-
partition DH products. Every selected subset yields exactly one vector.
The secp256k1 controls reproduce 34 views, 60 point-component reconstructions
and 34 square-CDH embeddings. Forty-four exact finite-field rank calculations
corroborate the degree lemma; an intentionally dependent control violates
its independence premise and exposes an extra candidate scalar.

Scope excludes restricted choice families, additional nonlinear public
metadata, other native disclosure interfaces and general garbling algorithms.
It does not rule out the complete pointlock goal. No new full-message native
construction or setup benchmark is supplied.

Evidence: **inspected** proof and the pinned primary proposition,
**locally-reproduced** field/curve controls. Deployment: **unclassified**.
Script/witness/hint/stack/opcode/transaction metrics are N/A for this host
experiment. Existing native pointlock metrics are unchanged.
