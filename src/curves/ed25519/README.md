# Ed25519 affine transition research kernel

## Question and comparison objective

Can fixed-base Ed25519 scalar multiplication be represented as a sequence of
point transitions whose arithmetic fits both a roughly 4,000,000-byte
Tapscript budget and Bitcoin's 1,000-item combined main/alt-stack limit?  This
kernel minimizes auxiliary hint items first, then generated bytes, while
retaining exact field identities over `p = 2^255 - 19`.

## Construction

For current affine point `(x,y)`, fixed non-identity point `(a,b)`, and claimed
next point `(x',y')`, set:

```text
tau = d*a*b*x*y
A=x+y, B=x-y, A'=x'+y', B'=x'-y'
C+=a+b, C-=b-a, K=(d*a*b)^-1
```

The verifier checks:

```text
x*y       - K*tau     = 0 mod p
A' + B'*tau - A*C+    = 0 mod p
B' + A'*tau - B*C-    = 0 mod p
```

Each relation combines its convolutions into a 51-coefficient accumulator.
Terms above bit 255 are folded immediately by multiplying by 19. The final
identity is traversed from its high coefficient down, deriving every radix-32
carry from one quotient. There are therefore exactly **three logical auxiliary
hint stack items per point transition**: `q0`, `q+`, and `q-`. In every
standalone wrapper below all three coexist at entry and are included in the
strict combined main-plus-alt-stack peak. `K`, `C+`, `C-`, coordinates, and
`tau` are authenticated data, not hints.

The proved R0 schedule uses three-digit limbs for `x*y` and 13 four-digit
limbs for `K*tau`, with K as the table-built left operand. This exact operand
orientation matters: the radix coefficient representation, and hence q0,
changes if K and tau are swapped even though field multiplication commutes.
The hostile-input proof bounds the accumulator by 1,900,945,648 and every
four-byte arithmetic intermediate by 1,967,441,867; q0 is signed 23-bit.

The byte-minimized R+/R- schedule constructs one 32-entry table for each tau
limb and routes `tau*x' - tau*y'` to R+ and `tau*x' + tau*y'` to R-. The normal
negative products use 17 three-digit A/B limbs and signed-21-bit q+/q-. The
smaller proved variant uses a `[4x6,3x9]` 15-limb partition only for `-A*C+`
and `-B*C-`; its maximum arithmetic intermediates are 1,987,147,066 and
1,935,550,777 respectively, and all three quotients are signed 23-bit. No
14-limb negative-product layout passed the hostile-input interval bound.

The direct-q boundary needs **zero additional range-check bytes**. Once all
coefficient sources have their documented certification and hostile-input
bounds, an accepted recurrence is exactly the integer equation `H=q*p`;
because p is nonzero, q is unique, and the proved H interval itself implies
the stated signed bit width. A witness item longer than four bytes aborts when
q reaches the first numeric opcode under consensus rules. Minimal ScriptNum
encoding is policy rather than consensus, so a non-minimal at-most-four-byte
alias represents the same q and does not weaken the identity check. A packed
quotient codec may still enforce canonical fixed-width encoding for witness
shape and malleability, but that is a separate scalar-boundary cost.

The unified signed/identity wrappers add exactly 59 raw bytes. Their two

`ed25519_signed_transition_benchmark`. The 330-item direct-constant peak is
the maximum for positive/negative; identity peaks at 300.

## Montgomery slope-chain candidate

The successor model and executable kernel use the Curve25519 Montgomery
coordinates `v^2=u^3+486662*u^2+u`. For a transition from the previous running
point to `u_i` by selected point `(a_i,b_i)` with slope `lambda_i`, it checks:

```text
lambda_i^2 = u_previous + a_i + u_i + 486662
lambda_i*(a_i-u_previous)
  + lambda_previous*(a_previous-u_previous) = b_i+b_previous
```

The first continuity relation instead binds
`lambda_1*(a_1-u_0)=b_1-v_0`. Thus each transition packet needs two packed
trace fields (`u_i` and `lambda_i`, 16 circuit-data items) and exactly **two
quotient-hint stack items**. The byte-aligned H16 challenge schedule has 44
transitions, so all 44 packets coexist at script entry: 704 trace-data items
plus exactly **88 quotient hints**, for **792 raw witness entry items**. There
are no separate scalar words in the witness. The carrier schedule predecodes
29 challenge-side q items and repacks their scalar bits into eight words while
restoring the q items. The witness starts response-first/challenge-last so
those carriers are shallow. A full item-accurate synthetic router starts with
all 792 items, returns 800, and reaches a strict combined peak of 813 items.
Its raw/unoptimized fragment is 25,231 bytes; a 2,032-byte zero-growth block
transpose then produces challenge-first/response-last execution order. The
complete strict probe exceeds the centralized policy's 32 KiB cutoff and
therefore uses no optimizer.

A separate G29 q-free schedule uses the same 704 packed trace-data items, supplies
the scalar in eight canonical compressed-u32 words, and derives both bounded
relation quotients inside every kernel. Its exact entry is
`challenge_trace[16] | response_trace[28] | scalar[8]`: **712 data items and
zero auxiliary hint items**, all coexisting at entry. Quotient derivation uses
only accumulator coefficients `h0..h4` to recover the unique signed-22/23-bit
residue before checking the complete radix-32 carry relation. This removes the
carrier router, metadata decoders, and packet transpose; it does not weaken the
full relation check.

The executable kernel consumes table/state `a` and `u` fields as 16 centered
`[4x3,3x13]` limbs and linear-only `b` and `v` fields as nine staggered
`[4,6x7,5]` limbs. The first fragment therefore has 66 claimed circuit-data
items plus two hints (68 complete local inputs), returns 41 state items, and
has 783 future/scalar/transcript items preserved below it. The second/chained
fragment has 82 claimed circuit-data items plus two hints (84 complete local inputs),
returns the same 41-item state, and has 766 items preserved below it. All
local items and preserved items coexist at their respective fragment entry.

The quick correlation-free bound model matches the kernel's sparse
coefficient representation. The square product uses `[4x12,3x1]`; sparse u/a
fields and continuity products use `[4x3,3x13]`; sparse b/v fields use nine
staggered limbs `[4,6x7,5]` at offsets
`0,4,10,16,22,28,34,40,46`. The symmetry-specialized square groups equal
cross terms before the pseudo-Mersenne fold, so its coefficient vector is
field-equivalent rather than byte-for-byte identical to the generic bilinear
accumulator. Its five low coefficient absolute bounds are
`[1,819,568,938, 1,982,956,800, 1,982,956,800, 1,982,956,800,
1,664,190,000]`; q lies in `[-3,404,320,3,631,275]`, reverse carry is at most
63,966,197, and maximum verifier arithmetic is 2,046,918,304. This leaves
100,565,343 below the positive ScriptNum limit and still fits signed 23 bits.
The regular-continuity q interval remains
`[-3,686,931,3,686,931]`, with coefficient/reverse-carry/maximum-arithmetic
bounds 2,072,011,424 / 66,330,611 / 2,122,579,552 and 24,904,095 of headroom.
The first one-product continuity relation is narrower. Accounting for a
sign-routed selected `b` limb as the union
`[-16*S_w,16*S_w]` gives coefficient 1,590,195,040, q in
`[-1,843,466,1,843,466]` (signed 22-bit), reverse carry 50,483,722, and maximum
arithmetic 1,615,479,104. The superficially simpler `[6x8,3]` b/v grouping is
unsafe at 2,746,042,313 maximum arithmetic; its width-six limb at coefficient
zero overlaps the product's largest coefficient.

Sign routing negates each selected `b` limb literally. If the grouped limbs
reconstruct the chosen centered integer `r(v)`, their limbwise negatives
reconstruct `-r(v)` exactly and therefore represent the field element `-v`
modulo `p`; they need not be the backend's canonical centered representative
of that residue. The regular relation was re-bounded with both selected and
previous `b` limbs in the full `[-16*S_w,16*S_w]` union. Its existing
2,122,579,552 maximum arithmetic and 24,904,095 margin are unchanged. The
largest sign-routed six-digit limb is 554,189,328, so literal `OP_NEGATE` also
fits four-byte ScriptNum arithmetic. Because folding replaces `32^51` by 19
modulo `p`, a true field relation over these literal representatives still
reconstructs an exact multiple of `p` and admits the quotient checked by the
kernel.

hint, witness, and output-state APIs. `literal_negative()` models the table's
per-limb sign routing exactly. The canonical-`BigUint` convenience functions

not in general byte-for-byte equivalent to passing the table's negated limbs:

### G32 hybrid-u5 zero-hint successor

The current schedule changes the response partition to 32 groups: all widths
are eight except lower positions 21, 25, and 29, which are seven. Together
with sixteen independent challenge bytes this gives 47 transitions. The
parity-correct top response leaf starts at `U-[K_127]A` without the G29
initializer's extra `T`; the 47 following `T` translations still end in the
`-U+H` coset.

Every ordinary trace packet is the same sixteen packed items. The terminal

## Security and composition obligations

- All unpacked field vectors are hostile unless the caller has already checked
  digit ranges and the unique canonical radix-32 encoding.
- Runtime `K,C+,C-` must be bound to the same authenticated table entry and
  scalar digit.  The transition equations do not authenticate table selection.
- The positive-only kernels exclude the identity because geometric K is
  undefined there. The controlled wrapper accepts only the normalized
  synthetic identity tuple `K=C+=C-=1`, `negative=nonzero=0`; the
  authenticated table supplies that tuple and `nonzero`, while the validated
  scalar callback derives `negative` from the same zero digit.
- The starting point must be valid, fixed points must be on Ed25519, exceptional
  denominator/identity handling must be proved for the complete schedule, and
  the final signature layer must enforce canonical encodings and the required
  subgroup/cofactor rules.
- In the q-free linked schedule, BLAKE3 copies packed R before those words are
  certified. The final derived challenge transition must execute against and
  certify the exact untouched originals; the hash helper alone is not a safe
  raw-witness boundary.
- All hints and trace items in a complete leaf coexist at witness entry. The
  affine kernel's three-hint count and the slope kernel's two-hint count cannot
  be analyzed independently of packed trace data, selected constants,
  accumulator items, and decoder scratch.
- Quotients must be generated for the exact limb partition and operand
  orientation used by the selected wrapper. A field-equivalent reordered
  convolution can differ by an integer multiple of p and fail the carry chain.
